## Functional checks

- Production build, light 390/1440: hero link opened `/rem/demo`; the embedded reader loaded without page errors or horizontal overflow. Three morning cards were present on the home state. The first card opened Mara's page; its numbered source list included `#src-6`.
- Recall: the answer began hidden. Keyboard focus plus Enter revealed it and exposed the answer; a separate 390px touch-context run tapped the reveal button successfully. The morning card and its `src-4` citation route both worked by keyboard and by touch at 390px.
- Return paths: the first Tab reached the visible `Back to REM overview` link; Enter returned to `/rem`. `Try with your notebook` reached `/rem#start`.
- Dark OS scheme, 390px: the actual reader changed to its dark palette; the sample bar remained legible and the page did not overflow.
- Static sample: no external script tags, no private home/temporary paths, and a frozen invented notebook. The released reader has no live mailbox connection from this route.
- Build gates: `npm audit --audit-level=moderate` reported zero vulnerabilities; lint had zero errors and 155 pre-existing warnings; blog source check, TypeScript, Webpack build, blog build check, and all-page SEO check passed locally. CI and Vercel deployment must still pass before publication.
- Not tested here: a real user's overnight pass, assistive technology traversal through the iframe, and mobile browsers beyond Chromium. The iframe has an accessible title, but a screen reader pass remains useful before treating this as a general accessibility benchmark.
